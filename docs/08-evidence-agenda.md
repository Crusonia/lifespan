# 08 · The evidence agenda

The benchmark's biggest finding so far is an inversion: **the slice of the gap with the most potential (metabolic health, upstream) has the weakest evidence on hard outcomes, and the levers with the strongest evidence act on smaller slices.** Closing that inversion is the most valuable thing anyone can do for the debate.

This page lists what evidence would move the numbers, in priority order. It is written so that anyone (a payer, a state, a health system, a food company, a research group, an investor) can pick an item up.

## Priority 1 · Pin the frontier and the gap (v0.1)

| Question | Data that answers it | Who has it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| US and peer HALE, on one consistent basis | WHO GHO, IHME GBD | Public |
| HALE by income quintile and by county | NHIS / BRFSS self-rated health with NCHS life tables; IHME county estimates | Public, needs assembly |
| Gap decomposition G1–G5 | GBD cause-of-death and YLD data; NCHS multiple cause of death | Public, needs analysis |

## Priority 2 · Calibrate the PreChronic transitions (v0.2)

| Question | Data that answers it |
| --- | --- |
| Annual Healthy → PreChronic and PreChronic → T2D transition rates by age | NHANES with linked mortality; longitudinal EHR and lab panels |
| Remission rate from PreChronic, with and without intervention | DPP / DPPOS, real-world DPP program outcomes |
| How long effects persist after an intervention stops | DPPOS long-term follow-up; GLP-1 discontinuation cohorts |

## Priority 3 · Test the upstream levers on hard outcomes

The trials that would move food levers from grade C to B or A.

| Design | What it would establish |
| --- | --- |
| **Randomized food-environment pilots** with metabolic endpoints (HbA1c, fasting insulin, TG/HDL) at 6–24 months | Whether a food lever moves PreChronic flows, and by how much |
| **Stepped-wedge rollouts** of produce prescriptions or procurement changes across sites | Effects at real-world scale, with a comparison group |
| **Outcome contracts** that pay only for measured change on the scoreboard | Whether paying for healthy years changes provider and supplier behavior |
| **Linked purchase and biomarker data**, with consent | Dose-response between what people buy and how their metabolic state changes |

Minimum standard for any pilot to count as evidence here: pre-registered endpoints, a comparison group, biomarker measurement at baseline and follow-up, and publication of null results.

## Priority 4 · Coverage and cost

| Question | Data that answers it |
| --- | --- |
| Healthy-year effect of coverage by age, income and condition | Medicaid expansion and natural-experiment literature, re-expressed in healthy years |
| Health spending by metabolic state and age | MEPS; claims data |
| Program cost per participant at scale | State and federal program reports |

## How to contribute evidence

Open an [evidence submission](https://github.com/Crusonia/lifespan/issues/new?template=evidence.yml) with the source, the effect estimate and its uncertainty, the population and the outcome measured. Data offers start with metadata and permissions. **Never post individual health records.**
