# 09 · Score a proposal

Anyone can submit any health proposal to be scored: a bill, a candidate platform, a think-tank plan, a state program, a payer model. Left, right or neither.

## What gets scored

1. **The five measures** in [01](01-scoreboard.md), at 10 and 30 years, with ranges.
2. **The deviation from the frontier:** the share of each gap slice (G1–G5 in [05](05-plans-vs-the-frontier.md)) the proposal closes, and what it leaves on the table.
3. **The guardrails:** financial protection, climate and land, capacity.
4. **What can't be estimated yet**, and why.

## How to submit

Open a [scoring request](https://github.com/Crusonia/lifespan/issues/new?template=score-proposal.yml) with:

- the proposal's name and a link to its primary source document;
- which levers it pulls (coverage, cost-sharing, food, drugs, payment, other);
- its own claims about cost and outcomes, if it makes any;
- any evidence you think should be used.

## The rules

- **Same rules for everyone.** The scoring method, evidence grades and model version are the same for every proposal scored against a given release.
- **Score the proposal as written.** Not its best or worst possible implementation. Where implementation is ambiguous, the score shows a range.
- **Show the work.** Every score links to its inputs, its model run and the evidence grade of each link.
- **Scores can be challenged.** Anyone can open an issue against a published score. Corrections are versioned, never silently edited.
- **No endorsements.** A score is a measurement, not a recommendation to vote, fund or oppose.

## Status in v0.1

The method is published; modeled scores begin in v0.2 (see [06](06-model.md)). Until then, submissions are collected, triaged and given a preliminary read against the evidence tables.
